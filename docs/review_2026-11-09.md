## The three things that matter most
1. **He has never played the real game.** There is no `default.json` save. All of his play (about 2.5 hours of active time since Nov 5) is in the Playtest 2 save, and Playtest 2 makes every chase add/subtract. He has caught all five snakes and opened every habitat having answered 200 add/subtract problems and **zero** counting, place value, time, money, or multiplying problems. Playtest mode is also why pens overflow (14 raccoons: caught animals come back) and why 168 stars sit unspent.
2. **The speed rule is holding him at one level.** To level up he must be right 5 of 6 *and* average under 8 seconds. On Nov 9 he did 62 "6 + ? = 13" problems at 83% right, but his middle time was 17 seconds, so he stayed on that level for over an hour. That is the "he will get bored" problem, already happening. One long pause (he walked away for 136 seconds on one problem) also drags the average.
3. **Two of the five sessions were fast guessing.** On Nov 7 and Nov 8, first tries were 50% right with middle answer times of 1–2.5 seconds on adding problems. I can't tell who was playing (it may have been his little brother). Either way the game let it happen: the level dropped from 5 to 2 over 12 minutes and 11 of 18 animals got away. Predators will hit this kind of play hardest, so it needs a design answer first (see "What this changes in the plan").

## Top 10 to fix (ranked)
1. **Move him to the real game** (see 1 above). Copy his Playtest 2 progress into his real profile once, with rescues beyond a map's real spots dropped, or start him fresh. Chris's call.
2. **Speed rule** (see 2 above). Cap each answer's time at about 20 seconds before averaging, give "find the missing number" its own longer limit, or drop speed from leveling up and keep it only for Race sprints.
3. **Not enough levels, and a big jump between add/subtract levels 4 and 5.** The robot hit the top of counting and add/subtract in 30 minutes. Level 4 is within 20; level 5 is "add within 100" (57% right for him). Nothing in between: no adding tens, no two-digit plus one-digit, no regrouping practice. Add two or three levels there. Subtraction within 20 is still his weakest (37–58%).
4. **The Meadow is far too easy for a 2nd grader.** Grade 2 starts counting at level 4: "Which number is more?" with two buttons, 2 or 8. A guess is right half the time. Counting tops out at kindergarten, so the Meadow never becomes real practice for him.
5. **The ranger in a chase is always the default red-haired ranger,** not the look he picked (`chase_screen.gd:479` uses `Ranger.DEFAULT_SHEET`; the map uses `RangerLook.sheet(GameState.look)`). Small fix.
6. **Stars have nothing to buy.** 20 decorations cost 180 stars in total; he earned 139 in one sitting and spent 36.
7. **Facts run out before levels do.** Every animal has 4 facts, but level 5 takes 10 care visits, so visits 5–10 show nothing new. He cares little anyway: 5 care visits against 34 chases on Nov 9.
8. **The game is left running for hours** (233 and 821 minute sessions). Mastery is only counted on "Save & rest" or closing the window, and he has mastered only 2 levels. The session timer (already planned) fixes this.
9. **`tools/summarize_playtest.py` crashes on a log with a cut-off last line** (the Nov 7 log, from a force-quit). Skip unreadable lines and say how many were skipped.
10. **You and Henrito still look alike, the raccoon is still red, every hub fence looks the same.** Already in the art steps.

## Code review
Overall: in good shape for its size (8,100 lines of game code, 329 tests). Logic is separated from screens the way `CLAUDE.md` asks, tuning numbers are in JSON, and the save code is careful (temp file, check, backup, never overwrites a save it can't read). No crashes or data-loss bugs found.

Findings, most important first:
- **Wrong ranger in chases** (Top 10 #5).
- **Saving is heavier than it needs to be.** Each first-try answer saves, and one catch saves three times in a row (the answer, the rescue, the stars). Each save reads and checks the old file, writes a temp file, reads it back, reads the old file again, then renames twice. Fine on this PC; the first thing to fix for the web build. Simplest: save once when a chase or care visit ends.
- **Renaming an animal id silently deletes its rescues.** `GameState._restore` skips rescues whose animal isn't in `animals.json`, and the next save writes the shorter list. Keep unknown rescues in the save instead of dropping them.
- **"Later (Esc)" skips a hard problem for free.** Leaving a chase before answering records nothing, so the same level comes back with a new problem. Not a bug today; with predators it becomes the way to dodge misses, so the danger meter must count a walked-away chase.
- **The chase's bonus star ignores an earlier escape.** After "Chase again" the miss count starts over, so a retry with no misses pays the no-miss bonus. Harmless.
- **Race timer runs during the 0.9 second pause between problems** and while a hint is showing: about 3 of the 30 seconds are not his to use.
- **Mastery isn't used by the game any more** (habitats open by snakes). It is still computed and saved for the future parent report. Keep, but it is the least-tested path in real life because sessions rarely end properly.
- **Big scene scripts.** `habitat_map.gd` (707 lines) builds the map, HUD, gates, snake hunt, and runaways. It works and is tested, but the art step ("look" per habitat) and predators both land here. Before adding them, move the HUD (banner, stars, mode button) into its own small script so the two steps don't collide.
- **Tests:** scripts with no test of their own that carry real logic: `word_problem.gd`, `hint_text.gd`, `ranger_look.gd`, `snake_screen.gd`. The others are covered by another file's tests (e.g. `test_profiles.gd` covers `game_state.gd`).
- **Plan check for predators:** losing a trip's rescues must also lower `pen_repairs_shown`, or the hub will never replay those fence repairs when he catches the animals again. Add to step 8's test.

## Design review (from the screenshots)
- **Title:** logo and cards on flat green. No animals, no art. This is the first thing he sees.
- **Ranger setup:** clear. Back and Start are dark on dark.
- **Hub on arrival:** every pen is the same broken fence on the same grass. Pen names are small pixel text (10 px) next to large HUD buttons. The top row of pens sits under the HUD. The two red-haired boys in the middle are easy to mix up.
- **Chase:** reads well; big prompt, big buttons. "Later (Esc)" still sits on the track like part of the scene. The catch meter (three gray dots, top right) has no label and is far from where he is looking.
- **Maps:** the Meadow looks good. The Desert is the Meadow with yellow ground: green leafy bushes, meadow flowers, and a pine-tree border. The Mountains are the Meadow, full stop: green grass, sunflowers, no rock or snow. The chase track is the same green path in every habitat.
- **Animals on the map** are 16 pixels and several don't read: the snow leopard is a thin gray shape, the eagle a dark blob. In the Book, the pack's lizard face looks like a man with a beard and a monocle.
- **Fact card:** two lines of text in the middle of an empty beige page with a small face in the corner. This is the best place in the game for a real photo.
- **Hub at the end:** pens are roomy with 2–3 animals each; nothing overflows in the real game. The "To the Mountains" sign sits under the Explore button.
- **Kid text:** stays calm and kind everywhere I looked. Nothing says wrong, bad, or fail.
- **Learning design:** five skills exist with 5–6 levels each and good Common Core tags, but each animal only ever asks its habitat's skill, so a habitat he has cleared stops giving that practice except through two runaways per visit.

## What this changes in the plan
The plan's steps stand. Suggested changes to their order and content:
- **New step right after this one: "Real game, right speed."** Move him to the real profile, fix the speed rule, fix the chase ranger, fix the log tool. All logic, small, and it makes every later playtest mean something.
- **Step 13 (more levels) moves up, before predators.** Add the in-between add/subtract levels and harder Meadow counting (skip counting, counting on from a number, comparing to 100). Predators punish misses, so the levels have to fit him first.
- **Predators and guessing.** Decide before step 8: fast wrong answers (under 2 seconds) should fill the danger meter the same as any miss, and the predator's escape problems should need a real answer (no two-button problems). Otherwise a guesser gets chomped constantly, or guesses through the escape.
- **Step 7b (pen limit)** is mostly a playtest-mode effect; it becomes small once he is on the real game. Still worth doing for babies and golden animals later.
- **Step 7a (facts):** the current facts are accurate and kid-friendly. The problem is the count (4), not the quality. Add facts rather than rewrite.
